# Changelog — Semi-Finished Product

## [0.1.0] — 2026-06-03

- Initial specification.
- Abstract base k-type for traceable physical pieces of material (sheets, coils, billets, bars).
- Ontology class: `bfo:Object`.
- Relations: `made_of` (`schema:material`) to `material`; `supplier` (`schema:supplier`) to `organization`.
- Custom properties: lot / heat number, receipt date, quantity.
